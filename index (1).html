About this concept

ReFind AI is a technology concept designed to make public lost-and-found systems faster, smarter and easier to use — from metro stations and airports to malls, parks, offices, libraries and events.

Its key idea is simple: AI finds the likely match, private verification protects ownership, and a two-sided handover flow confirms the item actually reached its owner.
